# Future Academy Zimbabwe: Parent Information Handbook

> DEMO DOCUMENT. Future Academy Zimbabwe is an imaginary school. Every name, fee, number and address below is made up for a demo.

## About the school
Future Academy Zimbabwe is a co-educational day school in Harare for children from Early Childhood Development (ECD) to Form 4. It follows the Zimbabwe primary curriculum and, in secondary, Cambridge IGCSE. Class sizes are capped at 25. The motto is "Learn today, lead tomorrow."

## School day
- Gates open 06:45. Lessons run 07:30 to 13:30 (ECD to Grade 3) and 07:30 to 15:00 (Grade 4 to Form 4).
- After-school clubs (chess, football, art, coding) run 15:15 to 16:15 on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Clubs cost USD 30 per term.

## Admissions
1. Book a school visit (see "Visits").
2. Submit the application form with the child's birth certificate and last school report, and pay the once-off application fee of USD 25.
3. The child sits a short assessment (Grade 1 and above) or a play session (ECD).
4. The admissions office confirms in writing within 10 working days. Only the admissions office can confirm a place.
5. To secure a place, pay the once-off registration fee of USD 100.

Entry ages: ECD A turns 3 in the year of entry; ECD B turns 4; Grade 1 turns 6.

## Fees (USD, per term)
| Level | Fee per term |
|---|---|
| ECD A and ECD B | 380 |
| Grade 1 to Grade 3 | 450 |
| Grade 4 to Grade 7 | 520 |
| Form 1 to Form 4 | 680 |

- Once-off fees: application USD 25, registration USD 100.
- Fees are due by the end of the second week of each term.
- Payment methods: bank transfer or EcoCash to the school account (details are on the invoice, never sent over WhatsApp).
- A sibling discount of 10% on the lower fee applies from the second child. Any other discount, payment plan or late-payment arrangement is decided by the bursar only.

## Uniform
Maroon blazer, grey trousers or skirt, white shirt, maroon tie. Uniform is sold at the school shop, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 15:00.

## Transport
The school runs a bus from Borrowdale, Avondale and Mt Pleasant. Bus fee: USD 60 per term.

## Visits
School visits run Monday to Friday at 09:00, 11:00 or 14:00 and last about 45 minutes. There are no visits on weekends or public holidays. Visits are booked through the admissions office.

## Health and safety
The school has a sick bay with a nurse on duty during school hours. Parents must tell the school about allergies and medication on the registration form. In an emergency the school calls the parent first.

## What the school does not offer
Boarding is not available. Swimming lessons are not offered.
